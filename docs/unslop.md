# Unslop Review

Use this reference for final review of substantive prose changes or a tone-focused review, including
README text, doc comments, and contributor guides. A typo or link correction does not need this
pass. Apply it to changed prose and enough surrounding text to judge continuity.

## Claims and Word Choice

- Replace praise such as "robust" or "seamless" with observable behavior. Rankings and claims of
  importance need a stated comparison or consequence.
- Check vague verbs such as "handles" or "ensures": can readers identify the operation, result, and
  relevant conditions? Name those details when the verb hides them.
- Remove ornate language and invented labels that add concepts without explaining the system. Keep
  established Git and Rust terms; avoid rotating synonyms for the same thing.
- Name sources behind attributed claims. Qualify unsupported certainty, precise-looking numbers, and
  broad guarantees. Replace generic caveats with the actual uncertainty or untested condition.

## Tone and Sentence Patterns

- Watch formulaic contrasts such as "not X, but Y," stacked negations, and self-answered questions.
  State the behavior directly unless the contrast resolves a real misunderstanding.
- Cut suspense, teacher patter, performative candor, and apologies when they delay the useful point.
  Preserve ordinary warmth where it fits the audience.
- Question empty transitions, emphatic adverbs, and trailing phrases that announce significance
  without explaining a consequence.
- Check repeated sentence openings, decorative triples, forced analogies, and dramatic punctuation.
  Keep useful rhythm and punctuation; revise patterns that make different ideas sound
  interchangeable.

## Structure and Local Voice

- Replace page narration with the subject: "This section explores repository ownership" needs an
  explanation of who owns which state.
- Remove repeated introductions, summaries, and adjacent restatements. Keep headings for substantial
  topics and lists for actual enumerations.
- Reconnect choppy fragments when readers need causality, conditions, or tradeoffs. Separate shifts
  from mechanism to history or uncertainty so the status of each claim remains clear.
- Compare terminology, formality, heading style, and formatting with nearby accepted prose. Remove
  chat residue, placeholder citations, and commentary addressed to the drafting requester.

## Review Judgment

These patterns prompt inspection; they are neither a banned-word list nor evidence of authorship.
For each finding, identify the passage and what becomes vague, exaggerated, repetitive, or harder to
understand. Repair wording before deleting useful explanation. Preserve reasons, qualifications,
examples, and caller obligations, and never fabricate specifics to improve tone. Accept wording that
works in context. Finish when the changed prose is accurate and readable; stylistic uniformity is
not a completion requirement.
